Online Rehab Coaching is for injured athletes outside Chicago, or anyone who cannot get into the room but still wants an expert in their corner. Online Rehab Coaching is coaching and education, not physical therapy or medical care. It does not include diagnosis, medical management, or hands-on treatment, it does not create a provider–patient relationship, and it does not replace an in-person provider. If you have a condition that needs to be examined, diagnosed, or treated, or your symptoms are severe or worsening, please see a licensed provider in person. Your coach is a Doctor of Physical Therapy working in a coaching role, not practicing across state lines, and if your case needs to be seen in person we will tell you and help you find care where you are.
