Rehab Coaching that goes where you go, guided by a Doctor of Physical Therapy.

Online Rehab Coaching is for injured athletes outside Chicago, or anyone who cannot get into the room but still wants an expert in their corner. It puts a Doctor of Physical Therapy alongside you as your coach, over a committed program rather than an open-ended subscription you renew and forget. They build your program, review your progress every week, and adjust as you go, all as coaching and education rather than hands-on treatment.

Every plan starts with a remote intake $149, a video-based session where we learn your history, watch you move, and build your program around what we see. After an initial 3- or 6-month program, you can keep going month to month with an Ongoing Monthly Subscription at $349/month.

Online Rehab Coaching is coaching and education, not physical therapy or medical care. It does not include diagnosis, medical management, or hands-on treatment, it does not create a provider-patient relationship, and it does not replace an in-person provider. If you have a condition that needs to be examined, diagnosed, or treated, or your symptoms are severe or worsening, please see a licensed provider in person. Your coach is a Doctor of Physical Therapy working in a coaching role, not practicing across state lines, and if your case needs to be seen in person we will tell you and help you find care where you are.
