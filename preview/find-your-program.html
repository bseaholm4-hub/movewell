We are here to help you achieve your desired result, other clinics sell you sessions.

Most people think of physical therapy as a room you visit and an hour you pay for until the pain settles down. That version gets you out of pain and then stops, right when the work that keeps you better should be starting. Getting out of pain is the beginning of the job, not the end of it, and what builds and holds progress is the capacity you rebuild in the weeks between your visits. We do that through a small set of committed programs, each matched to where you are and how far you want to go. Programs start at $1,695.

Every path begins with a Recovery & Performance Assessment, 90 minutes that includes baseline testing so we can scope the right program for you, at $249.
