Get Started

We are here to help you achieve your desired result, other clinics sell you sessions.

Most people think of physical therapy as a room you visit and an hour you pay for until the pain settles down. That version gets you out of pain and then stops, right when the work that keeps you better should be starting. Getting out of pain is the beginning of the job, not the end of it, and what holds is the capacity you rebuild beyond that, with progressive programming and consistency in the weeks between your visits. We do that through three distinct programs, each matched to where you are and how far you want to go.

Restore

For Acute and Minor Injuries

2 Months · 6 Sessions

For the mostly healthy athlete dealing with a minor sprain or strain, or someone finishing the last stretch of a rehab they started elsewhere. Focused work to close the gap and get back to training at full capacity.

What's included

  • ✓Eight weeks of progressive programming in the CoachRx app, updated as you adapt
  • ✓Direct messaging access to your provider between every visit
  • ✓Six one-on-one sessions, more frequent early on and spacing out as your body adapts to the work you put in
  • ✓Video demonstrations and coaching cues for each exercise
  • ✓Entry and exit testing on our VALD force plates, with a report card so you can see exactly how far you came
$1,795

Pay in full, or monthly across the two months.

Rebuild

For Serious and Recurring Injuries

4 Months · 12 Sessions

For anyone dealing with a recurring issue or coming back from something that took real capacity away. Four months to rebuild the strength and tissue tolerance to not just feel better, but genuinely trust your body under load again.

What's included

  • ✓Sixteen weeks of progressive programming in the CoachRx app, updated as you adapt
  • ✓Direct messaging access to your provider between every visit
  • ✓Twelve one-on-one sessions, more frequent early on and spacing out as your body adapts to the work you put in
  • ✓Video demonstrations and coaching cues for each exercise
  • ✓Entry, week-eight, and exit VALD testing, each with its own report card
  • ✓A monthly progress review with your provider so the plan changes when you do
  • ✓Criteria-based progression so you advance based on what the testing shows, not guesswork
$3,195

Pay in full, or monthly across the four months.

Return to Performance

For Return to Sport and Long-Horizon Performance

6 Months · 24 Sessions

For a full return to sport, later-stage post-operative rehab, or anyone with a longer runway who wants to come back stronger than they started. A structured six-month plan with clear testing checkpoints and criteria-based exit.

What's included

  • ✓Programming in the CoachRx app for the full six months
  • ✓Direct messaging access and provider coordination throughout
  • ✓Twenty-four one-on-one sessions, more frequent early on and spacing out as your body adapts to the work you put in
  • ✓VALD testing roughly every eight weeks with a report card after every test, plus clearance testing before you're released
  • ✓A monthly progress review with your provider
  • ✓Criteria-based return-to-sport clearance, decided on your data rather than the calendar
$6,000

Pay in full, or monthly across the six months.

Every path starts with a Recovery & Performance Assessment $249, a 90 minute visit that includes baseline testing with objective measures as indicated (dynamometry, force plates), giving us a clear baseline to measure your progress against. That assessment is where we learn your presentation and recommend the right program for where you are. Programming runs between every visit through the CoachRx app. When your formal plan of care wraps up, graduates can carry the momentum forward with Continuity Care, ongoing programming at $299/mo with one-on-one sessions at $169 each as clinically indicated, month to month.
Get started

Your sessions are scheduled across your program and stay valid for its full length plus a short buffer. Your programming keeps running the whole time, so you stay on track even on the weeks we do not see you in person.

What your program actually looks like

Your visits are heaviest early on, while loading is new and your provider needs to see how you respond. As the weeks go on and your programming builds momentum, they spread out. You finish more independent, not more reliant.

Why a program and not visits

An injury doesn't resolve in the room with us. It resolves in the weeks between visits, when you're doing the work. A program is priced on getting you to a defined finish line: in-person sessions, your programming for every day we're not together, testing to prove it worked, and direct access to us the whole way. Visits are one part of that.

What you're actually paying for

The plan is built from the sessions you need, the programming that runs between them, and the testing that shows whether you're progressing. The price covers the whole window, start to finish, so nothing is metered and nothing gets held back. The longer the plan, the more it covers: more sessions, more programming, and more testing across the whole window.

What the assessment gives you

Sixty to ninety minutes, including baseline testing on our VALD force plates. You leave knowing what's driving the problem, where you stand against normative data, and which program fits. That's the case for spending $249 before you've committed to anything else.
